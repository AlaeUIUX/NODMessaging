# NOD app review: gaps and quick wins

Updated 14 October 2026, after your feedback on the first review.

## Gaps compared with WhatsApp, iMessage, Telegram and Slack

1. **Pinned messages:** pinning saves a flag, but nothing in the app ever shows pinned messages.
2. **Threads:** groups only have quoted replies. Teams coming from Slack expect threads.
3. **Safety:** there's no block or report, admins can't remove other people's messages, and there are no join requests.
4. **Inbox organisation:** no archive, folders or mark-as-unread. Drafts are saved but never shown in the inbox.
5. **Expression:** reactions are limited to 27 fixed emoji, there are no stickers or GIFs, you can't zoom photos, and there are no video messages.
6. **Forwarding messages:** a message in one chat still can't be forwarded to another. Things from Mind can now be shared into chats; ordinary messages can't.

## Quick wins that build on what's already there

- **Link previews in chat:** Mind already reads links: paste one and its title fills in, and YouTube or Vimeo videos play inside the app. Chat messages don't do this yet: a link in a message is plain text. The same lookup could show a preview card, and a YouTube link could play right in the thread.
- **A pinned-messages bar** at the top of each chat, so pinning finally shows something.
- **"Remind me about this" on any message**, using the Reminder card that already exists.
- **A draft marker in the inbox**, so a chat with an unsent draft says "Draft:".

## Done since the last review

- **Send to chat from Mind:** a collection or a single item can be shared into a chat, with view, edit and copy permissions.
- **Mind reorganised:** collections all sit on one level, grouped by tags instead of stacks, and chat saves go to one Unassigned collection with a Sort flow.
- **Dashboard:** it covers the "what needs you" summary the first review suggested.

## Decided against, or out of scope

- **Voice:** voice notes stay a pre-loaded demo.
- **Message notifications:** iOS handles these.
- **Search:** searching chat names and last messages is enough.
- **Encryption:** an engineering matter, not part of this design review.
- **Flashcard review mode:** flashcards are too narrow (mostly for learners) to build more on.
