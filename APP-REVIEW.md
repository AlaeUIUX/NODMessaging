# NOD app review: gaps and quick wins

## Gaps compared with WhatsApp, iMessage, Telegram and Slack

1. **Voice:** voice notes only play back a pre-loaded fake. Recording and calls both say "coming soon".
2. **Message notifications:** banners only exist for reminders, plan stops and tasks. A new message never notifies you, and there's no quick reply. The permission prompt promises "mentions and replies".
3. **Search:** inbox search only matches chat names and the last message, even though its placeholder promises messages, users and items. You can't search across chats, or across chats and Mind together.
4. **Forward and share:** there's no Forward, and nothing in Mind can be sent into a chat.
5. **Pinned messages:** pinning saves a flag, but nothing in the app ever shows pinned messages.
6. **Threads:** groups only have quoted replies. Teams coming from Slack expect threads.
7. **Safety:** there's no block or report, admins can't remove other people's messages, and there are no join requests.
8. **Inbox organisation:** no archive, folders or mark-as-unread. Drafts are saved but never shown in the inbox.
9. **Expression:** reactions are limited to 27 fixed emoji, there are no stickers or GIFs, you can't zoom photos, and there are no video messages.
10. **Trust:** every chat says "Messages are end-to-end encrypted", but nothing is encrypted. Either change the wording or plan for real encryption.

## Quick wins that build on what's already there

- **Link previews in chat:** the link-reading route Mind now uses (`/api/unfurl`) can power them, and YouTube links could play inline with the same player.
- **Send to chat from Mind:** share an item or a whole collection into a conversation, so Mind works both ways.
- **Small chat additions:**
  - a pinned-messages bar at the top of each chat
  - "Remind me about this" on any message, reusing the Reminder card
  - a draft marker in the inbox
- **Flashcard review mode:** cards already track "still learning", but there's no way to review them. A review session would make Mind a daily habit for language learners.
- **A "Needs you" home:** the app already tracks open cards for the blue dot, the Live tab and the Dashboard. Turn that into a daily summary, like "3 things need you today".
