Analytics v1 — what changed

Each area of your life gets its own card and its own way of showing data, so you can take it in at a glance before reading a single row.

  1. Money shows this month's spending as a running line against last month's (dashed), so "am I spending more or less" is visible without doing any maths. Owe and owed sit together with the action beside them.
  2. Needs you is one number with a segmented bar showing what kind of thing is waiting. The top three items can be acted on right there (Vote, Going, Pay) without leaving the page.
  3. Tasks uses a dotted ring for progress. Checklists uses a dot grid where each dot is one real item, ticked or not.
  4. This week is a 7-day strip with a dot for each thing booked that day, then a short timeline with times first.
  5. Activity is a dot chart where each dot is one message. Today is in NOD cobalt. Tap a column to see its count.

Colours: stone neutrals plus NOD cobalt as the one accent. Amber only means money you owe, and red only means overdue.
