# Shot list: WhatsApp on iPhone

Record these in order, one action per clip, in the "NOD test" group or the 1:1 chat with your colleague (setup in [README.md](README.md)). Each one covers rows in [inventory.md](inventory.md) where NOD is Different or Missing.

- **Start each clip** a second before the action and **stop** a second after the end state.
- ✋ = a gesture. Do it slowly. Filming the phone with a second phone helps, but is optional.
- **Don't rename files.** Claude matches clips to shots by the order they were recorded. Delete a bad take on the phone before redoing it.
- If a step doesn't exist in your WhatsApp (versions differ), skip that step and carry on.

## Batch 1: every day (14 clips, about 25 minutes)

| # | Where | What to do | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Group | ✋ **Hold a colleague's message.** Pause on the menu, open More… and pause (Reply privately, Translate, Remind me), go back. Tap "+" in the reactions, scroll the emoji, search "pizza", pick one. | C1 C2 C5 C14 C18 |
| 2 | Group | ✋ **Hold one of your own messages**, pause on the menu, tap Info and stay on it. Go back, then **swipe that message left** to see whether it opens Info too. | C5 C7 |
| 3 | 1:1 | ✋ **Swipe a colleague's message right** to reply, send "ok". **Swipe one of your own messages** to reply, send "ok". **Tap the quote** in a reply to jump to the original. **Double-tap** a message and pick a reaction. **Tap a reaction** your colleague left under a message. | C6 B9 C3 C19 |
| 4 | Group | **Forward a message:** hold, Forward, pick two chats (search for one), send. Show where the "Forwarded" label appears. | C10 |
| 5 | Group | **Select several messages:** hold, More… (or Select), tick three, then open the Forward and Delete options without finishing. | C11 |
| 6 | 1:1 | **Delete:** send "test 1", delete it for everyone. Send "test 2", delete it for me, then tap Undo if it appears. | C9 |
| 7 | 1:1 | ✋ **Voice note**, ringer on (so sounds are recorded): hold the mic 3 s, slide left to cancel. Hold again and slide up to lock. Speak 5 s, pause, listen back, then send. | D3 D4 D12 J6 |
| 8 | 1:1 | **Play your colleague's voice note:** tap play, switch speed to 2×, drag along the waveform, then go back to the chats list while it plays. Show the transcript if there is one. | E7 E8 E9 |
| 9 | 1:1 | **Send photos:** tap the camera button by the field, take one, add a caption, send. Then "+" › Photos, pick 3, flick through the editor (crop, draw, HD), send. Show the album in the chat. | D2 E1 E2 E3 |
| 10 | 1:1 | ✋ **Open a photo from the album:** pinch to zoom, double-tap, swipe to the next one, show the buttons (reply, forward, share), then swipe down to close. | E4 E5 |
| 11 | 1:1 | **Paste a link** (a YouTube video), wait for the preview while typing, send. Tap the card. Do the same with the news article. | B16 D9 |
| 12 | Chats list (pick the "NOD test" list) | ✋ **Swipe a chat right**, mark unread. **Swipe left**, open More…, close it, then Archive. Open Archived, unarchive. **Hold a chat** to see the preview, close it. Type a draft in a chat and go back to the list. | A3 A4 A5 A6 A8 |
| 13 | Group | ✋ **Scroll up a long way** so the date floats at the top, then tap the scroll-down button. Open the keyboard and **drag the messages down** to close it. If there's an @ button for a mention, tap it. | B3 B5 B12 |
| 14 | 1:1 | **Stickers, GIFs, emoji:** tap the sticker button in the field, browse stickers, switch to GIFs, search "thanks", send one. | D6 E14 |
