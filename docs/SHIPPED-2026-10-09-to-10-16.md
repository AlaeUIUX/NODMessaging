# Shipped: Fri Oct 9 to Fri Oct 16, 2026

Six commits, all on `main` (live). Some later Mind changes replace earlier ones; the current
state is noted where it matters.

---

## Fri Oct 9: Dashboard charts, Explore and boards

**Dashboard**
- Analytics is renamed **Dashboard**, with a four-squares icon.
- Widgets draw real charts (Recharts): sized to fit, with values on hover and touch.
- A **Chart** choice per widget: Area, Line, Bars, Donut, Stacked, Gauge or Blocks.

**Boards**
- **↑ / ↓ arrows** step a task to the group above or below.
- Folded groups share the space, so every group shows a task, each with its own **+N** expand button.

**Explore**
- **Shelves** per collection you keep ("Because you collect 🇩🇪 German"), plus "Popular on NOD":
  one card at a time, with the next one peeking.
- Collections show one per row.
- **No more + buttons:** hold a card for its menu, like a message.
- A smaller "Add to Mind" pill, and smooth sideways scrolling.

**Mind**
- A Grid / List switch for collections.
- Name and icon in one field.
- Search grouped by type, showing where each result lives.
- **Publish a collection to Explore.**

**Group Mind**
- Items open over the whole screen and can be held.
- The editor's view switcher is no longer hidden under the header fade.

## Mon Oct 12: Mind content types and colours

- **Links:** a Paste button; the title fills itself from the page.
- **Videos:** title, channel and thumbnail come from YouTube or Vimeo; tapping one opens it already playing.
- **Books:** one search across Apple Books and Open Library (title, author, ISBN or link), with covers and details.
- **Images and files:** "Choose from your photos" and "Choose a file"; drop or paste on a computer.
- **Palettes like Coolors:** change, lock, copy, generate (Space), undo and redo.
- **An iOS-style date picker**, used in Mind, Plans and task due dates.
- **New colours:** priority is red, orange, blue and stone; "soon" is no longer yellow; the chart
  palette has proper dark-mode steps.

## Tue Oct 13: Sharing and a pre-launch bug sweep

**Mind sharing**
- Share a collection or an item with people or by link.
- Send it into a chat as a live card.
- Ask for access, edit a shared copy, or make your own copy.

**Inside collections**
- Search, Filter (tags, date changed, type) and a ⋯ menu.
- Tags work across all of Mind.

**Dashboard**
- Small and medium widgets fit every phone.
- Bars are blue gradients.
- The "own page" mode is gone; a tapped item opens in its chat.

**Fixes**
- Link previews are safer: the server checks the address it actually connects to, so a link can't
  point it at a private network.
- A "50%" link no longer crashes a contact page.
- Shared-editing rules are tighter: per-editor drafts, and access re-checked on save.
- Invite links work only once.
- Reactions can't get stuck, and photo download saves the photo shown.

---

## Wed Oct 14: Mind simplified

- **No more stacks or sections.** A collection holds its items side by side, and **tags** group
  them. Old stacks became tags automatically.
- Every collection has a **quick-add field**: notes, links, "word = meaning" flashcards, #tags.
- **Unassigned + Sort** replaces the "To sort" pile in every collection:
  - Saves with no collection go to **Unassigned**.
  - **Sort** moves them to collections in one go.
  - A chat you sorted from sends its next saves straight to that collection.
